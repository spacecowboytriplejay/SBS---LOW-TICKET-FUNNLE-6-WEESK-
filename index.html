the protocol.

Six weeks. Every week ends with something built, shipped, and live. This is assembly, not information.

Week 1 · Foundation

The offer & the operator

  • You will lock in the AI-native business model, and the economics of one operator plus AI
  • You will pick and sharpen your offer, your buyer, and your promise
  • You will build the operator mindset: energy, focus, and why volume beats perfection
You ship: your offer, written and public.
Week 2 · Distribution

The personal brand engine

  • You will nail the positioning and pin architecture that makes strangers follow
  • You will master the content formats that built billions of views: hooks, story, open loops
  • You will record once and distribute everywhere with the repurposing system
  • You will master the yap, engineered: talking to camera is the fastest trust-builder on the internet, because the brain reads unscripted speech as honesty. We train the reps, the hooks, and the delivery until you hold attention effortlessly, even if all you want to be is a world-class yapper
  • Never want to be on camera? Fine. You will deploy our agentic faceless content strategies and run the machine without your face. The brand is the system, not one person, and we have built more than one way to feed it
You ship: profile rebuilt + your first content live.
Week 3 · The Agentic AI Machine

AI-native systems & agents

  • You will assemble your content factory: research, script, edit, and repurpose with AI
  • You will switch on automations and agents that run lead capture and follow-up without you
  • You will command the stack that makes one person run like a team of five
You ship: one working automation + your content engine running.
Week 4 · Autonomous Acquisition

The pipeline

  • You will install the acquisition sequence: content warms the market, systems open the doors, automation carries people to the call. Nobody wants to be sold to; the sequence gets you bought
  • You will deploy our autonomous connection engine, an AI we built that finds the right people for you, buyers, partners, investors, vets the match, and opens the conversation on your behalf, while a manual SDR team is still copy-pasting its 40th message of the day
  • You will turn content into inbound, run outbound that does not feel gross, and take DMs to booked calls
  • You will get found inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI answers with AI search and GEO
  • You will install CRM discipline: every conversation tracked, every follow-up automated
You ship: 10 real conversations started, tracked.
Week 5 · The Close

Sales & conversion

  • You will run the audit-style call: diagnose, map, prescribe. Never pitch
  • You will master objection handling, pricing confidence, and collecting payment
  • You will turn attention into money without a sales team, yet
You ship: your first close, or 3 booked calls.
Week 6 · Advanced Scale Systems

The scale plan

  • You will run paid acquisition and the self-liquidating funnel model
  • You will know when to hire, what to delegate, and what breaks at each level
  • You will leave with your 90-day roadmap to a machine that compounds without you
You ship: your 90-day scale plan. Graduation.
